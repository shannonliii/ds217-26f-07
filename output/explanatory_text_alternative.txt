Line chart of the percentage of patients meeting the weekly exercise goal of 150 minutes (y-axis, %) at follow-up visits 1 to 4 (x-axis) for two cardiac rehab programs. Home-based patients (orange dashed line, squares) rose from 58% at visit 1 to 70% at visit 4. Center-based patients (blue solid line, circles) started similarly at 57% and rose to 79% at visit 4, a 9-point gap over home-based. Limitation: patients chose their program, so the gap does not show that one program causes more exercise, and each percentage covers only the patients seen at that visit, about 45 per program, so a 9-point gap could also come from chance or from which patients returned.
